The Felix was the first watch Drop designed itself. Drop, the enthusiast marketplace once called Massdrop, is better known for keyboards and headphones. Felix is Latin for "lucky".

It follows the field-watch formula: a black dial with an inner 24-hour scale, C3 Super-LumiNova on the hands and numerals, and white and orange triangular markers. The dial rises in a gentle scallop at its edge instead of sitting flat. The bead-blasted steel case has a screw-down crown and is water resistant to 100 m.

Drop offered it with either an STP 1-11 or a Sellita SW200 Swiss automatic movement, both with 26 jewels, 28,800 vph and about 39 hours of power reserve. This one has the solid caseback engraved with Drop's 49-Mile Drive logo, so the movement stays out of sight. Drop has since become Corsair's gaming-collaborations site and no longer sells the Felix.
