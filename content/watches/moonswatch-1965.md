The MoonSwatch is Swatch's Bioceramic, quartz version of the Omega Speedmaster Moonwatch. This one is named for 1965, the year NASA qualified the Speedmaster for crewed missions.

The year is on the dial: the counters at 10 and 2 run to 19 hours and 65 minutes. The dial is white with the vintage Omega logo, set in a grey Bioceramic case with a black Bioceramic tachymeter bezel. The case is 42 mm across and 47.8 mm lug to lug, and the watch comes on a grey VELCRO® strap.
