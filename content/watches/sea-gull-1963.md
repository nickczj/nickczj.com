Sea-Gull built the original under Project D304 in the early 1960s as a chronograph for the Chinese Air Force. This 37.3 mm reissue keeps the original proportions, the red and blue hands and the gilded star, and adds a sapphire display back so the column wheel is visible.

The ST1901 (ST19) is Sea-Gull's own hand-wound, column-wheel chronograph movement, descended from that original Air Force calibre. Sea-Gull quotes a power reserve of roughly 37–45 hours.
