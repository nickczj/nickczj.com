The Record is Longines' chronometer line: every movement is tested and certified by COSC, the Swiss chronometer institute. The L888.4 calibre uses a monocrystalline silicon balance spring. Silicon is non-magnetic, so the spring isn't thrown off by magnets the way a steel one can be.

This reference pairs a silver sunray dial, applied indexes and pink hands with a steel case and bracelet capped in 18k rose gold. A sapphire display back shows the movement.
